# Community code of conduct

## Our standards

We want project spaces to be useful, evidence-oriented, and welcoming. Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on artifacts, claims, and behavior;
- distinguish facts, inferences, uncertainty, and personal preferences;
- provide reproducible evidence when making technical claims;
- respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure;
- accept corrections and maintainer decisions without harassment;
- avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, abusive, or deliberately disruptive conduct.

These standards apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private agent or repository data, knowingly unsafe instructions, spam, and sustained disruption of technical collaboration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and based on observed conduct rather than identity or viewpoint.

For platform abuse, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For a security issue or sensitive data exposure, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting details publicly.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by common open-source community standards and the [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
